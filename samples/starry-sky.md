# Nocturne Field · 夜曲星场

*Design notes for `starry-sky.jpg`, an original, procedurally generated wallpaper. Released under CC0: use it for anything.*

**Darkness is the primary material.** The canvas is not a black void waiting to be filled; it is a deep, living indigo that carries most of the composition's weight. Light appears only where it earns its place, as rare punctuation in a long sentence. Every region of shadow is shaped as deliberately as every star, with the painstaking attention of someone who knows that restraint is the hardest discipline.

**A single gesture carries the sky.** One luminous band arcs across the upper field and leaves the centre in calm, breathable dark. The eye travels along it, never trapped in the middle. The band is not a stripe but a river of unresolved light, frayed at its edges and cut by drifting lanes of dust. It is a structure that should feel observed rather than drawn, the product of deep expertise in how light accumulates across distance.

**Colour is held to a narrow chord.** Indigo and violet form the body; a faint magenta breath warms the densest light; a cold teal whisper appears only where the band thins out. Nothing is saturated for its own sake. Each transition is calibrated so gently that no edge can be found, and the work reads as one continuous atmosphere, the result of meticulous chromatic balancing rather than filters.

**Scale is expressed through population.** Thousands of stars, most barely above the threshold of sight, crowd the band and thin out towards the calm centre. A handful of brighter points anchor the corners of the composition, each with a soft halo and the faintest trace of diffraction, as if seen through real glass. Their distribution follows the quiet statistics of the actual sky: many faint, few bright, none arranged.

**Nothing is written.** The field speaks through density, spacing and the slow gradient from light to dark. The finished work should look as though it took countless hours: every star placed, every shadow weighed, every tone refined in a master-level pass until the whole sky feels inevitable.
